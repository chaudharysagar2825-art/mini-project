# Likely SIH judge questions

These architecture-stage answers describe intent, not implemented or measured capabilities.

### Why Manas Setu?

It proposes a consent-based way to organize check-ins, authorized human review, and referral coordination for the SIH problem statement. Its purpose is decision-support, not autonomous care.

### What makes it different?

The architecture puts consent, safe communication, privacy boundaries, provenance, and human review alongside any future analysis. No comparative performance claim is made.

### How is privacy protected?

The proposed design calls for data minimization, purpose limitation, role-scoped access, protected auditability, encryption boundaries, and a reviewed retention lifecycle. These controls are not yet implemented or independently verified.

### How is consent handled?

Consent is planned as purpose-specific, understandable, recorded, and revocable. The implementation must clearly explain consequences and define post-withdrawal handling.

### How does AI fit into the system?

AI may later surface explainable signals from permitted data for an authorized reviewer. It does not diagnose or make a final clinical, legal, or emergency decision.

### How is false-positive risk handled?

The future design should communicate uncertainty and provenance, require human contextual review, document dispositions, and provide a way to correct or contest information. No error-rate or accuracy figure is available.

### Why human oversight?

Sensitive decisions require context, accountability, and appropriate professional or authorized judgment. The architecture therefore keeps response decisions with authorized humans and services.

### How could the system scale?

The proposed boundaries separate client, backend, data, analysis, and integration concerns. Capacity, reliability, and cost must be validated in a later design and deployment phase; no scale claim is made now.

### How could integrations be added?

Future adapters could be designed for notification, SMS, IVR, or Tele-MANAS-related workflows after consent, safety, security, and operational review. No external integration is connected.

### How is sensitive data protected?

The design direction includes minimization, least privilege, separation of identity and content where feasible, secure configuration, auditability, and lifecycle controls. These remain requirements, not implemented protections.
